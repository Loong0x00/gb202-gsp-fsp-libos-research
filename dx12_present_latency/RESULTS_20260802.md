# Minimal D3D12 presentation latency probe — 2026-08-02

## Purpose

Test whether the generic Wine/VKD3D-Proton D3D12 submission and presentation path can explain the approximately 8.65 ms aggregate delay inferred from the Black Myth: Wukong 854x480 ultra-performance 4x-MFG control.

The program creates an 854x480, three-buffer, flip-discard D3D12 swap chain; records a clear and two GPU timestamp queries; submits and presents with tearing allowed; and measures CPU call duration, buffer reuse waits, GPU execution, and complete loop cadence with `QueryPerformanceCounter`.

## Exact Black Myth runtime

The Black Myth Proton log reports:

- GE-Proton10-28
- vkd3d-proton build `4d4abaa230fed54`
- NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090, driver 610.43.3

### Three frames in flight

- Observed loop rate: 7607.044 FPS
- Loop interval: 131.457 us mean
- `Present`: 115.193 us mean, 145.100 us p50, 195.805 us p95
- D3D12 submit: 0.605 us mean
- Buffer reuse wait: 0.109 us mean
- GPU timestamp around clear/barriers: 3.191 us mean

### Serial completion after every frame

- Observed loop rate: 6037.009 FPS
- Loop interval: 165.645 us mean
- `Present`: 40.283 us mean
- Submit: 0.884 us mean
- Queue signal plus fence completion wait: 107.486 us mean
- GPU timestamp around clear/barriers: 3.194 us mean

## Controls

Proton 11.0 with vkd3d-proton build `ef20c02c61e10fd` produced 7778.436 FPS in the triple-buffered test and 7191.665 FPS in the serial test.

The system Wine 11.14 path was not representative: it reported a compatibility adapter name of `NVIDIA GeForce GTX 470` and periodically blocked `Present` for approximately 10.15 ms, producing 292.280 FPS. It was therefore excluded from attribution to the Black Myth path.

## Conclusion

The exact GE-Proton/VKD3D-Proton path used by Black Myth adds approximately 0.115 ms of mean `Present` call time in this minimal test, not milliseconds. Even a deliberately serialized submit/present/GPU-completion loop completes in approximately 0.166 ms. Generic D3D12-to-Vulkan translation and presentation therefore cannot explain the approximately 8.65 ms aggregate MFG-cycle penalty by themselves.

The remaining candidates are inside or immediately around the DLSS-G path: Streamline interposition, DLSS-G inference and resource synchronization, hardware flip metering, game integration, or an interaction between those components and Proton. A minimal Streamline/DLSS-G integration is required to separate DLSS-G itself from the game's integration.
